Md. Yosuf

Economics graduate, development economist and published researcher in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

Co-author of two peer-reviewed articles on students’ environmental awareness, published in 2026, and presenter of a conference paper on smartphone addiction. Research so far covers food prices, public budgets, maternal health and road safety in Bangladesh, using survey fieldwork and analysis in SPSS and Stata. Founder of YS Scholaria, an academic and career service. Preparing for graduate study in development economics.
